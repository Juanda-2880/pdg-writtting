# Common Pitfalls and What to Avoid in Research Objectives

A reference guide for auditing and refining research objectives in theses, dissertations, and research proposals. Use this document during drafting or review to identify and eliminate fatal formulation flaws.

---

## 1. Confusing Objectives with Methodological Activities / Tasks

### The Mistake
Drafting procedural steps, administrative tasks, or data-gathering activities as if they were research objectives. An activity is a means to an end; an objective is the cognitive or empirical end state itself.

| Category | Incorrect (Activity / Task) | Correct (Scientific Objective) | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Literature Review** | *"Revisar la literatura sobre redes neuronales convolucionales."* | *"Caracterizar el estado del arte de las arquitecturas de redes convolucionales aplicadas al diagnóstico por imágenes médicas."* | Reading papers is a study task, not a contribution or research finding. |
| **Data Collection** | *"Aplicar 200 encuestas a clientes de la empresa X."* | *"Determinar el nivel de satisfacción y fidelidad de los clientes de la empresa X."* | Administering a survey is a mechanical step; the objective is the resulting empirical insight. |
| **Interviews / Fieldwork** | *"Realizar entrevistas a expertos en ciberseguridad."* | *"Identificar los principales vectores de vulnerabilidad percibidos por especialistas en ciberseguridad."* | The interview is the vehicle; the identification of vulnerabilities is the objective. |
| **Tool Execution** | *"Correr simulaciones en MATLAB con diferentes parámetros."* | *"Evaluar el comportamiento dinámico del sistema de control ante variaciones paramétricas mediante simulación numérica."* | Running a script is an activity; evaluating system behavior is the intellectual target. |

> [!TIP]
> **The Test:** Ask *"If I perform this action, does it guarantee new knowledge or an evaluated result?"* If the answer is just "I will have collected data or read documents," it is an activity. Reframe it around the knowledge or outcome produced.

## 2. Using Multiple Verbs in a Single Objective (Compound Objectives)

### The Mistake
Stacking two or more action verbs in the same objective (e.g., *"Diseñar y construir..."*, *"Analizar, proponer y evaluar..."*).

### Why It Fails
- **Ambiguous Evaluation:** If the researcher designs the artifact but fails to evaluate it, was the objective met?
- **Unclear Scope:** It bundles distinct research phases into a single statement, obscuring timeline and methodological allocation.
- **Complex Verification:** Thesis evaluation committees cannot establish a single, clean metric of success.

### How to Fix
- **One action verb per objective.**
- If the actions belong to sequential stages, decompose them into separate specific objectives.
- If one verb merely describes the means or method, place it in an instrumental clause (*"mediante..."*, *"a través de..."*).

**Anti-pattern Example:**
> ❌ *"**Analizar** los requerimientos del sistema, **diseñar** una arquitectura en la nube y **evaluar** su rendimiento bajo estrés."*

**Corrected Formulation:**
> ✔️ **Obj. 1:** *"Identificar los requerimientos técnicos y funcionales del sistema bajo condiciones de alta concurrencia."*  
> ✔️ **Obj. 2:** *"Diseñar una arquitectura nativa en la nube orientada a microservicios."*  
> ✔️ **Obj. 3:** *"Evaluar el rendimiento y la tolerancia a fallos de la arquitectura diseñada mediante pruebas de estrés."*

## 3. Vague, Subjective, or Unmeasurable Verbs

### The Mistake
Selecting verbs that describe internal cognitive states, intentions, or subjective reflections rather than observable, verifiable outcomes.

### Verbs to Ban
- ❌ *Conocer* (To know)
- ❌ *Comprender* / *Entender* (To understand)
- ❌ *Aprender* (To learn)
- ❌ *Estudiar* (To study)
- ❌ *Reflexionar* / *Pensar* (To reflect / think)
- ❌ *Sensibilizar* / *Concientizar* (To raise awareness — unless designing a measurable educational intervention)
- ❌ *Profundizar* (To deepen knowledge)

### Why They Fail
An examining committee cannot objectively verify whether the author has "understood" or "deepened" their understanding. A scientific objective must culminate in demonstrable evidence, models, correlations, or evaluated data.

### Replacement Verbs
| Vague Verb | Actionable & Measurable Replacements |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Comprender* | **Analizar**, **Interpretar**, **Explicar**, **Correlacionar** |
| *Estudiar* | **Examinar**, **Caracterizar**, **Diagnosticar**, **Contrastar** |
| *Conocer* | **Identificar**, **Describir**, **Categorizar**, **Determinar** |
| *Profundizar* | **Sistematizar**, **Modelar**, **Evaluar exhaustivamente** |

## 4. Scope and Hierarchy Mismatches

### The Mistake
1. **Specific Objective exceeds the General Objective:**
   - *Example:* General Objective is *"Describir los factores de deserción universitaria..."* (Descriptive level), while Specific Objective 3 is *"Validar un modelo predictivo de machine learning para predecir la deserción..."* (Predictive/Evaluation level).
   - *Rule:* The cognitive depth of specific objectives must never exceed the cognitive depth of the general objective.
2. **General Objective is too narrow:**
   - The general objective covers only one small part of what the specific objectives actually accomplish.
3. **Redundant Specific Objectives:**
   - Two specific objectives that measure the same phenomenon using slightly different wording.

## 5. Objectives Formulated as Value Judgments or Prejudged Answers

### The Mistake
Pre-judging the result within the objective itself, introducing confirmation bias before testing hypotheses.

- ❌ *"Demostrar que la metodología ágil X es superior a la metodología tradicional en equipos remotos."*
- ✔️ *"Comparar la productividad y la tasa de entrega de valor entre la metodología ágil X y la metodología tradicional en equipos remotos."*

- ❌ *"Probar que el fármaco Y elimina eficazmente la infección sin efectos secundarios."*
- ✔️ *"Evaluar la eficacia y la incidencia de efectos secundarios del fármaco Y en pacientes con..."*

## 6. Quick Audit Checklist

Before finalizing any objective, verify against these six gates:

- [ ] **Gate 1 (Single Verb):** Starts with exactly one infinitive action verb.
- [ ] **Gate 2 (Not an Activity):** Represents an intellectual or empirical result, not a bureaucratic or operational chore (e.g., not "leer artículos" or "hacer entrevistas").
- [ ] **Gate 3 (Verifiable / Measurable):** Does not use vague verbs (*conocer*, *comprender*, *estudiar*). The output can be measured or evaluated against real evidence.
- [ ] **Gate 4 (Syntactic Completeness):** Answers **What** (verb + variable), **Where/Who** (context/sample), and **How/Why** (purpose or method).
- [ ] **Gate 5 (Hierarchical Balance):** Specific objectives logically sum up to fulfill the general objective without exceeding its cognitive scope.
- [ ] **Gate 6 (Neutrality):** Avoids prejudging outcomes or advocating for a preferred conclusion.
