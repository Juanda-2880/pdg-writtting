# Thesis Writing Core Module

This module serves as the primary orchestration skill for scoping, structuring, drafting, and reviewing empirical theses, dissertations, undergraduate capstones (TFG), and master's theses (TFM).

---

## Purpose

Guides the author and AI agent through the overarching lifecycle of thesis development across 5 operational modes:
1. **Mode 0:** Define & Scope the Topic (Research question, gap, significance, feasibility).
2. **Mode 1:** Outline & Structure (Chapter-by-chapter and subsection-level breakdown).
3. **Mode 2:** Draft Sections (Claim $\rightarrow$ Evidence $\rightarrow$ Interpretation, factual Results vs. interpretive Discussion).
4. **Mode 3:** Review & Edit (Micro and macro editing passes, structural coherence).
5. **Mode 4:** Process & Planning (Chunking, cadence, and timeline checkpoints).
