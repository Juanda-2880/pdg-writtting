# Direct Quotations in Academic Writing (Citas Directas o Textuales)

A comprehensive guide for integrating, formatting, and attributing exact verbatim text from external sources in theses, dissertations, and research papers according to APA 7th edition standards.

---

## 1. Overview & Ethical Requirements

A **direct quotation** (*cita textual o directa*) reproduces the exact words of an author verbatim.

### Mandatory Citation Metadata
Every direct quotation must explicitly include three elements:
1. **Author(s)** (Surname / Apellido)
2. **Year of publication** (Año)
3. **Specific locator**: Exact page (`p. 45`), page range (`pp. 45–46`), or paragraph number (`para. 7` for online sources without pagination).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In empirical scientific theses, direct quotations should be used sparingly. Reserve them for:
> - Seminal definitions established by discipline founders or international bodies.
> - Exact legal, statutory, or regulatory text.
> - Qualitative data (e.g., verbatim interview extracts or participant transcripts).
> - Statements where the author's original phrasing is so singular that paraphrasing would distort its nuance.

## 2. The Two Categories of Direct Quotations

APA 7th divides direct citations into two strict structural classes based on word count:

```
                          ┌────────────────────────┐
                          │ Direct Quotation Check │
                          └───────────┬────────────┘
                                      │
                 ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────┐
                 ▼                                         ▼
         < 40 Words                                    ≥ 40 Words
   [Short In-Text Quotation]                      [Block Quotation]
   • Enclosed in double quotes ""                 • Freestanding indented block (0.5 in)
   • Integrated into paragraph prose              • NO quotation marks
   • Period placed AFTER citation                 • Period placed BEFORE citation
```

## 3. Short Direct Quotations (< 40 Words)

### Rules:
- Integrate directly into the active paragraph flow.
- Enclose the verbatim segment within double quotation marks (`"..."`).
- Do not insert an ellipsis (`...`) at the beginning or end unless the original author included one.
- The closing punctuation mark (period, comma, semicolon) appears **outside** and **after** the closing parenthetical citation.

### Formats & Examples:

#### A. Narrative Style (Emphasis on the Author)
> De acuerdo con Dijkstra (1972), "las pruebas de software pueden utilizarse para demostrar la presencia de errores, pero nunca para demostrar su ausencia" (p. 6), lo que resalta la necesidad de modelos de verificación formal.

#### B. Parenthetical Style (Emphasis on the Content)
> En el ámbito del aseguramiento de calidad, se sostiene que "las pruebas de software pueden utilizarse para demostrar la presencia de errores, pero nunca para demostrar su ausencia" (Dijkstra, 1972, p. 6).

---

## 4. Long Direct Quotations / Block Quotes (≥ 40 Words)

### Rules:
- Place on a new line as a freestanding block.
- Indent the entire block **0.5 inches (1.27 cm)** from the left margin (same as a new paragraph indent).
- **Do NOT use quotation marks.**
- Preserve normal document line spacing (double-space in APA 7th).
- If the block contains multiple paragraphs, indent the first line of each subsequent paragraph an additional 0.5 inches (1.27 cm).
- **Punctuation Rule:** The period or closing punctuation mark is placed at the end of the quoted sentence, **BEFORE** the final citation parentheses.

### Formats & Examples:

#### A. Narrative Block Quote:
> Al conceptualizar el impacto del acoplamiento en la evolución del software, Fowler y Beck (2018) argumentaron lo siguiente:
>
> > La refactorización no es una actividad aislada que deba programarse en el calendario de desarrollo como un evento extraordinario. Por el contrario, constituye una práctica inherente al flujo diario de programación orientada a mantener la comprensibilidad y la adaptabilidad de la base de código frente a requerimientos cambiantes. Cuando los equipos posponen sistemáticamente esta labor correctiva, la velocidad de entrega disminuye de forma exponencial. (p. 45)

#### B. Parenthetical Block Quote:
> La acumulación indiscriminada de soluciones temporales degrada la mantenibilidad del sistema:
>
> > La refactorización no es una actividad aislada que deba programarse en el calendario de desarrollo como un evento extraordinario. Por el contrario, constituye una práctica inherente al flujo diario de programación orientada a mantener la comprensibilidad y la adaptabilidad de la base de código frente a requerimientos cambiantes. Cuando los equipos posponen sistemáticamente esta labor correctiva, la velocidad de entrega disminuye de forma exponencial. (Fowler & Beck, 2018, p. 45)

## 5. Modifications and Permissible Alterations

When modifying direct quotations to fit your text:
1. **Omitting text (Ellipsis):** Use three spaced dots (`...`) to show where words have been omitted. Do not omit text if it changes the author's original meaning.
   - *"El algoritmo converge [...] en tiempo polinomial (Knuth, 2015, p. 88)."*
2. **Inserting explanations or brackets (`[...]`):** If you add words or clarify a pronoun, enclose the additions in square brackets:
   - *"El sistema [de microservicios] experimentó una degradación sustancial (Pérez, 2021, p. 14)."*
3. **Adding emphasis:** Italicize the words and immediately follow with `[énfasis añadido]` or `[emphasis added]`:
   - *"La seguridad en tiempo de compilación es *innegociable* [énfasis añadido] en sistemas de misión crítica (Smith, 2020, p. 302)."*
4. **Noting errors in original source (`[sic]`):** If the original contains a spelling or grammatical error, insert `[sic]` immediately after:
   - *"Los resultados confirman que el coheficiente [sic] de fricción fue nulo (Martínez, 2019, p. 12)."*

---

## 6. Audit Checklist for Direct Quotations

- [ ] Does the quotation include Author, Year, and specific Page/Paragraph locator?
- [ ] Is the quote under 40 words? (If yes: double quotes used, period placed after citation).
- [ ] Is the quote 40 words or more? (If yes: block indent 0.5 in., no quotes, period placed before citation).
- [ ] Is the quotation transcribed 100% faithfully to the original wording and spelling?
- [ ] Is every quotation immediately unpacked and analyzed in your own words rather than left hanging?
