# Paragraph Structure Module

This module provides structural architectures, typologies, opening strategies, and cohesion standards for drafting and reviewing academic paragraphs in scientific papers, engineering theses, and research reports.

---

## Purpose

A paragraph is the fundamental argumentative unit of academic prose. This module ensures that:
1. Every paragraph develops **exactly one central idea** through the tri-part model: *Fase Organizadora* (Claim/Topic Sentence) $\rightarrow$ *Contenido* (Empirical Evidence) $\rightarrow$ *Fase de Cierre* (Analytic Takeaway/Bridge).
2. Paragraph lengths adhere to academic norms (4 to 8 sentences, 100 to 250 words).
3. Writers can deliberately choose among established development archetypes and introductory styles suited to their section's rhetorical function.
4. Coherence and cohesion are maintained via the Given-New Contract, discourse markers, and clear antecedents.
